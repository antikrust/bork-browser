Say this and you're gone

One phrase per line. Anyone who says one of these is blocked outright — not just that message, but everything they already had on screen and everything they say for the rest of time. They land in Blocked people below, so it survives a reload; unblock there if you're feeling merciful.

Words and phrases

These hide the offending message only, leaving the person alone. One rule per line. Plain text matches anywhere in a message. Wrap a rule in slashes for a regular expression — /^first!*$/i — and it keeps exactly the flags you give it. Lines starting with # are ignored, so you can leave notes.

      Subscription tabs

      Tabs across the top of your subscriptions feed. Hover a video there, press TAG, and pick one: the channel goes in that tab and everything it posts from then on arrives under it. Tagging a channel rather than a video is deliberate — a feed turns over every few hours, so tagging videos one at a time would never be finished.

      Watch is the exception, and holds single videos. Putting one there saves it with YouTube's own Save to Watch later, so it is in their sidebar and survives everything. Taking it out again only drops the note kept here for the tab; the saving is theirs to undo.

      These tabs filter what the feed is already showing. They fetch nothing, so a video is reachable under a tab for exactly as long as YouTube keeps putting it in the feed.
