Say this and you're gone

One phrase per line. Anyone who says one of these is blocked outright — not just that message, but everything they already had on screen and everything they say for the rest of time. They land in Blocked people below, so it survives a reload; unblock there if you're feeling merciful.

Words and phrases

These hide the offending message only, leaving the person alone. One rule per line. Plain text matches anywhere in a message. Wrap a rule in slashes for a regular expression — /^first!*$/i — and it keeps exactly the flags you give it. Lines starting with # are ignored, so you can leave notes.
